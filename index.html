Since 2019, Projeckt Debs has worked across diverse civil engineering and construction fields, with a focus on transforming clients’ ideas into projects that stand the test of time.

“We build houses; we build people.”

The company is wholly Ghanaian owned and was incorporated in December 2019. Its work spans general construction, civil works, architectural design, project management, supervision, refurbishment and real estate development.

Its stated vision is to become a leading construction and service provider in local and international markets through technology, quality, speed, flexibility and competitive pricing.
